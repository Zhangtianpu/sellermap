Privacy Policy

How we protect and use data

Last updated: September 23, 2026. This policy applies to the SellerMap cross-border ecommerce navigation website.

1. Scope

SellerMap is a directory that helps users discover cross-border ecommerce websites and tools. This policy explains the information that may be processed when you visit the site and how that information is used.

2. Information we may process

The site does not require an account or ask you to submit personal information. Our hosting provider may record standard access logs such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, referring URL and timestamps for security, reliability and troubleshooting.

The site uses browser local storage to remember your light or dark theme preference. This setting remains in your browser and is not actively uploaded to SellerMap.

3. Google AdSense and third-party advertising

SellerMap uses Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure advertising based on visits to this and other websites.

You can manage personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings and review How Google uses advertising data.

4. Cookies and Google Analytics

SellerMap uses Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID: G-1KC28CWQ4F) to understand page views, traffic sources, device and browser types, engagement and website performance so we can improve the directory and user experience.

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar identifiers to process visit data. You can learn about available controls through the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on and review the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Third-party links

SellerMap links to many third-party services. Once you visit another website, its privacy policy, cookies and data practices apply. SellerMap does not control those third-party services.

6. Information sharing

SellerMap does not sell personal information. Trusted service providers may process limited data where necessary for hosting, security or advertising under their applicable terms.

7. Security and retention

We use reasonable measures to protect the website and its services, but no internet transmission is completely secure. Access-log retention depends on the hosting provider and legitimate security or legal requirements.

8. Children's privacy

SellerMap is intended for cross-border ecommerce professionals. It is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Policy updates

We may update this policy when website features, advertising services or legal requirements change. The latest revision date will be shown on this page.

10. Contact us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, email sellermap.service@gmail.com.
